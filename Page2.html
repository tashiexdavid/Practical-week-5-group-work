How Technology Can Improve Healthcare

Within the Healthcare system:

A key area in which technology has been particularly significant in the field of healthcare is medical diagnosis. advanced algorithms are able to analyze images recieved from MRIs, CT scans and X-rays, with an increasingly greater accuracy. this aids doctors in their ability to recognise diseases and abnormalities at an early stage, allowing for life saving medical treatment to take place sooner. The combined effect of having fewer wrong disgnoses and a faster resposnse to the correct diagnosis allows doctors to plan and excecute treatment plans sooner, therefore resulting in a higher chance of recovery.

Technology also makes it easier to store patient information and exchange it between different hospitals all over the country. Allowing patients to seemlessly switch between different gps when needed. It allows access to extensive medical history, all gathered in one place, making it easier to harness data science techniques over big data in order to recognise areas in the healthcar systems that could become more efficient and cost saving.

Personal Accountability:

The Development of wearable tech is another way in wich the advancement of computers has improved the quality of people's lives. examples of some wearable tech and how it is helping:

  • Smart watches
    • They can alert emergency services if it detects someone is having a heart attack
  • Sensor Rings
    • These track patterns in sleep, heart rate, blood pressure and other vitals, allowing users to recognise patterns and help detect underlying health conditions
